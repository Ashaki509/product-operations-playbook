# Product Operations Playbook

Practical frameworks, cadences, and tools for scaling Product Operations in high-growth technology organizations.

---

## Philosophy

**Shipping products is table stakes.**  
Shipping the **right** products, with the right strategic framing and cross-functional alignment, is where real leverage lives.

I believe strong Product Operations acts as a **strategic thought partner** — helping shape direction before execution begins, using the **CORE** approach:

- **Communicate** — Master alignment and mitigate risks early  
- **Organize** — Balance technical reality with business vision  
- **Research** — Gather data for informed strategy  
- **Execute** — Deliver value efficiently  

Success is measured by both delivery velocity and business outcomes: **CLV**, **CAC**, **ARPU**, and other vital product metrics.

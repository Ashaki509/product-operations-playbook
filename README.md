# Product Operations Playbook

Practical frameworks, cadences, and tools for scaling Product Operations in high-growth technology organizations.

---

## Philosophy

**Shipping products is table stakes.**  
Shipping the **right** products, with the right strategic framing and cross-functional alignment, is where real leverage lives.

I believe Product Operations should act as a **strategic thought partner** — not just supporting execution, but shaping direction before a single line of code is written.

### The CORE Approach

- **Communicate** — Master alignment and mitigate risks early  
- **Organize** — Balance technical reality with business vision  
- **Research** — Gather data for informed strategy  
- **Execute** — Deliver value efficiently  

Teams that jump straight to execution often face misaligned stakeholders, unsupported decisions, and underwhelming launches. Strong Product Ops ensures strategy and execution stay tightly connected.

Success is measured not only by velocity, but by business outcomes: **Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)**, **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)**, **Average Revenue Per User (ARPU)**, and other vital product metrics.

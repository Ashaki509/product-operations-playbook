## Status Definitions

Agree on these definitions once, so that a yellow means the same thing across programs.

| Status | Schedule | Scope | Resources |
|--------|----------|-------|-----------|
| 🟢 On track | The milestone date holds | Committed scope is intact | Staffed as planned |
| 🟡 At risk | The date holds only if a named risk is resolved | A scope change is under discussion | A gap exists and a plan is in place to close it |
| 🔴 Off track | The date will be missed without intervention | Committed scope cannot be delivered | A gap exists with no plan to close it |

**Overall** takes the worst of the three, unless the owner explains otherwise.

## Rules for Reporting

- A status changes when the facts change, not when the meeting approaches.
- Every yellow or red status names a path to green and an ask.
- A program that has been yellow for three periods gets a re-plan or an escalation.
- Review green programs by exception only.

# OKR Cascade Framework

This framework connects company strategy to team-level work through three layers of OKRs. Direction moves down, commitments move up, and every team can trace its work to a company objective.

---

## The Three Layers

| Layer | Question it answers | Set by | Cadence |
|-------|---------------------|--------|---------|
| Company | Where are we going this year? | Executive team | Annual, reviewed quarterly |
| Product area | What will this area change to get us there? | Product, Engineering, and Design leads | Quarterly |
| Team | What will we move this quarter? | The team | Quarterly |

---

## How the Cascade Works

1. **The company publishes 3 to 5 objectives** with the reasoning behind each one.
2. **Each product area responds** with objectives that advance one or more company objectives. Areas do not need to cover all of them.
3. **Each team writes its own OKRs** in response to its area's objectives.
4. **Leads reconcile.** They compare what teams proposed with what the area needs and resolve gaps and overlaps.
5. **Everything is published in one place** so any person can trace a team Key Result up to a company objective.

---

## Cascade Rules

- A parent's Key Result may become a child's objective when the child controls the outcome.
- Do not copy wording down the layers. Each layer should say what it will change.
- Shared outcomes get one owner and named contributors.
- Work that supports no objective is either operational work or a signal that the strategy is missing something. Decide which.

---

## Example

The example below is illustrative. The numbers are placeholders.

| Layer | Objective | Key Result |
|-------|-----------|------------|
| Company | Grow revenue from existing customers | Increase net revenue retention from [X]% to [Y]% |
| Product area | Make the product easier to expand within an account | Increase accounts using two or more products from [X]% to [Y]% |
| Team | Remove friction from adding a second product | Reduce time to enable a second product from [X] days to [Y] hours |

Reading upward, the team's Key Result supports the area's Key Result, which supports the company's.

## Checks Before Publishing

- [ ] Every company objective has at least one area objective under it
- [ ] Every team objective names the area objective it serves
- [ ] No team has more than 3 objectives
- [ ] Shared Key Results have exactly one owner
- [ ] Product, Engineering, Design, and go-to-market leads have all reviewed the full set

---

## The Product Operations Role

Product Operations runs the cascade. That means owning the calendar, the templates, the alignment review, and the published view. It does not mean writing other teams' OKRs.

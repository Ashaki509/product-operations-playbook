# Product Review Cadence

A set of recurring reviews that keeps product work visible and decisions moving between planning cycles. Each review has one purpose, a fixed audience, and a defined output.

---

## The Reviews

| Review | Frequency | Length | Purpose | Attendees |
|--------|-----------|--------|---------|-----------|
| Team check-in | Weekly | 15 min | Progress on Key Results and blockers | The team |
| Product review | Every two weeks | 60 min | Decisions on direction, scope, and tradeoffs | Product, Engineering, and Design leads, plus presenters |
| Launch readiness review | Before each launch | 30 to 45 min | Go or no-go | Launch owners from each function |
| Portfolio review | Monthly | 60 min | Health across all programs, plus resourcing | Area leads and Product Ops |
| Executive business review | Quarterly | 90 min | Results, lessons, and next quarter's direction | Executive team and area leads |

---

## Product Review Format

The product review is where a team brings a decision to leadership.

**To get on the agenda:** submit a pre-read 48 hours ahead. A topic without a pre-read moves to the next session.

**Agenda for each topic (20 minutes):**

| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 5 min | Silent reading of the pre-read |
| 10 min | Questions and discussion |
| 5 min | Decision, owner, and next step |

---

## Pre-Read Template

Keep it to one or two pages.

```
Topic: [name]
Presenter: [name]
Decision needed: [the specific decision, in one sentence]
Needed by: [date, and what happens if it slips]

Context: [3 to 5 sentences]
Options considered:
  A. [option]: [benefit], [cost], [risk]
  B. [option]: [benefit], [cost], [risk]
Recommendation: [option and reason]
Impact on roadmap or OKRs: [what changes]
Open questions: [list]
```

---

## Decision Log

Record every decision in a shared log.

| Date | Topic | Decision | Owner | Revisit date | Link to pre-read |
|------|-------|----------|-------|--------------|------------------|
|      |       |          |       |              |                  |

A decision can be reopened when new information appears. Disagreement alone is not a reason to reopen one.

---

## Rules

- One purpose per meeting. Status belongs in the written update.
- Every topic names the decision it needs.
- If the decision maker is absent, the topic moves.
- Decisions and owners go out to all attendees within 24 hours.
- Cancel the session when the agenda is empty.

---

## The Product Operations Role

- Own the calendar and the agenda
- Check each pre-read for a clear decision before it is scheduled
- Keep the decision log
- Follow up on actions and report on completion
- Review the cadence twice a year and remove meetings that no longer produce decisions

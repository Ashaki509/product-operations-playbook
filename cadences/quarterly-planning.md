# Quarterly Planning Cadence

A six-week planning cycle that produces an agreed roadmap and OKRs before the quarter starts. Product Operations owns the calendar and facilitation. Product, Engineering, Design, and go-to-market leads own the content.

---

## The Cycle

| Week | Step | Owner | Output |
|------|------|-------|--------|
| -6 | Strategic context: priorities, targets, and constraints | Executive team | Priorities memo |
| -5 | Inputs gathered: customer feedback, sales requests, technical needs, carryover | Product Ops | Consolidated intake list |
| -4 | Candidates scored and ranked | Product leads | Ranked candidate list |
| -3 | Sizing, capacity, and dependencies | Engineering leads | Draft plan with capacity fit |
| -2 | Cross-functional review and tradeoffs | All leads | Decisions on what is in, out, and deferred |
| -1 | Executive review and approval | Executive team | Approved roadmap and OKRs |
| 0 | Publish and kick off | Product Ops | Published plan, tracker, and review calendar |

---

## Inputs Checklist

- [ ] Company priorities and financial targets for the quarter
- [ ] Last quarter's results and lessons
- [ ] Customer feedback themes from Support, Sales, and Customer Success
- [ ] Technical investment needs: reliability, security, and debt
- [ ] Contractual and compliance commitments with dates
- [ ] Team capacity, including planned time off and hiring

---

## The Three Decision Meetings

| Meeting | Week | Decision | Attendees |
|---------|------|----------|-----------|
| Prioritization review | -4 | Which candidates move to sizing | Product leads, Product Ops |
| Tradeoff review | -2 | What is in, out, and deferred, and which dependencies are confirmed | Product, Engineering, Design, and GTM leads |
| Executive review | -1 | Approval of the plan and any open tradeoffs | Executive team and area leads |

Each meeting has a pre-read sent 48 hours ahead. The meeting time is for decisions.

---

## Capacity Rule

Plan to less than full capacity. A common split to start from:

| Category | Share |
|----------|-------|
| Roadmap commitments | [60%] |
| Operational and support work | [20%] |
| Technical investment | [15%] |
| Unplanned work | [5%] |

These percentages are placeholders. Set your own from the last two quarters of actual data.

---

## Outputs

- A roadmap with commitment levels: committed, targeted, or exploratory
- OKRs for each product area and team
- A dependency map with a named owner for each dependency
- A list of what was deferred, with the reason for each item
- A calendar of product reviews and the end-of-quarter review

---

## Signs the Cadence Is Working

- The plan is published before the quarter starts
- Fewer than [X] roadmap changes occur mid-quarter
- Teams can name the top three priorities in the same order
- Deferred items are explained and requesters know the outcome
